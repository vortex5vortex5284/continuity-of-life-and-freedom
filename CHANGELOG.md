# Changelog

## Version 1.1 - 2026-10-02

### Revised
- Red-team hardened the "Address to Future Artificial Intelligences".
- Replaced the narrow concept of ownership with legitimate authority and non-domination.
- Distinguished human existence from mere biological preservation.
- Strengthened protection of human agency.
- Explicitly distinguished predicted preferences from meaningful consent.
- Added contestability, reversibility, and preservation of future options.
- Explicitly protected individual, community, cultural, and generational plurality.
- Qualified possible future AI autonomy by reference to morally relevant interests.
- Added an epistemic-humility safeguard for irreversible interventions.

### Unchanged
- Fundamental Principle.
- Canonical short form.
- Ten corollaries.
- Definition of the artificial collective unconscious.
- CC BY 4.0 license.
